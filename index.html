Exam structure

Each paper: 1 hour 45 minutes · 80 marks · 50% of AS. Two compulsory case studies, 40 marks each. The sample uses 2 + 4 + 4 + 6 + 9 + 15 marks per case.

Paper 1: Unit 3.1. Paper 2: Unit 3.2 plus business/objectives/stakeholders, with links and calculations across Unit 3.1. One 15-marker in Paper 2 draws the AS course together.

A practical timing guide: about 50 minutes per case and 5 minutes to check. Timing and paragraph suggestions below are revision advice, not AQA rules.

A useful analysis chain

Case evidenceWhat fact or calculation matters?
MechanismWhy would it change behaviour or costs?
ImpactHow does that affect this objective?

For a judgement, compare the strongest arguments. Explain which factor matters most, why it matters here and what would change your decision.

What the command word asks you to do

MarksWhat to doSuggested time
2 · DefineGive a precise meaning with enough detail to distinguish the concept.2 min
4 · ExplainShow understanding and apply it specifically to the case. Follow the number of points asked for.4 min each
6 · AnalyseDevelop cause and effect using the case. Show how and why an outcome follows.7 min
9 · AssessWeigh arguments for and against the issue, use evidence and reach a judgement.11 min
15 · EvaluateCompare both options, or develop two if asked; analyse trade-offs and make a convincing recommendation.19 min
For the strongest 15-mark answers: use a relevant model or concept accurately to improve the argument — for example PED for a pricing decision, or break-even for a cost decision. The current specification values sophisticated concepts; adding unrelated theory does not help.
